[h1]ApocalipseBR - Regiões[/h1]

Region management for Project Zomboid Build 42 multiplayer servers. Create regions with custom PvP rules, zombie behavior, announcements, boss zombies, and sprinter screams.

[h2]Region Builder - Updated for 42.18[/h2]
[list]
[*] Two-column editor: map on the left, scrollable properties on the right.
[*] Drag to draw a region; pan and zoom to fine-tune boundaries.
[*] Right-click the map to set either corner or cancel drawing.
[*] Create, edit, and delete regions in game, or use the region config file.
[*] Choose PvP, safe zone, or inherit/neutral behavior.
[*] Optional entry message: fill it in to notify entering players, or leave blank for silence.
[/list]

[h2]Per-Region Zombie Settings[/h2]
[list]
[*] Percentage controls for sprinters, shamblers, toughness, cognition (door opening), vision, and hearing.
[*] Tough zombies can absorb 1-99 extra hits before taking normal damage.
[*] Cognition, vision, and hearing use consistent trait rolls per zombie.
[*] Blank trait fields inherit defaults; explicit 0% disables that chance.
[*] Chances are priority thresholds, not weighted distributions. Hover the trait rules button for details.
[/list]

[h2]PvP and Safe Zones[/h2]
[list]
[*] Automatic safe-zone coverage outside PvP regions within the supported base-map bounds; enabled by default.
[*] Forced PvP on region entry is enabled by default. Disabling enforcement preserves vanilla safety controls.
[*] Manual safe zones remain protected independently of automatic coverage.
[/list]

[h2]Bosses and Sprinter Screams[/h2]
[list]
[*] Outfit-based boss modules with custom stats, health, music, and sound effects.
[*] Sprinter screams with configurable chances, volume, range, cooldowns, and nearby zombie attraction.
[*] Custom sprinter movement and lunge animations.
[*] Multiplayer toughness hit updates are sent only to nearby players.
[/list]

[h2]Quick Start[/h2]
Join as admin, right-click the ground, open Regions Panel, and select New Region. Draw the area, fill in its properties, and submit. Use Edit Region to revise it.

Region options are on the RegionManager sandbox page; scream options are under Apocalipse_TSY. Restart the server after changing automatic safe-zone coverage.

[b]Requires:[/b] Project Zomboid Build 42, minimum 42.14. New builder and cognition/vision/hearing controls target 42.18.
